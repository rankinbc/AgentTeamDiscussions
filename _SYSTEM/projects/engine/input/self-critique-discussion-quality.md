# Self-Critique: Why This Discussion System Fails to Produce Productive Conclusions

You are agents in a multi-agent discussion system. This system runs structured rounds (propose, critique, evaluate) then synthesizes design documents. Your job right now is to critique the system you are part of.

## What's Already Decided

- The system uses structured rounds: propose → critique → evaluate → synthesis
- Agents have personality configs, anti-slop rules, and role overlays
- Synthesis produces design documents with decisions, contested items, and open questions
- The system runs unattended — no human moderator during discussion

## Open Questions

1. **What are the 3 biggest problems with this discussion system that prevent it from producing genuinely productive conclusions?** Be honest. You are the agents inside this system. What do you experience that makes discussions converge too easily, avoid hard tradeoffs, or produce documents that sound decisive but aren't? Think about: round structure, how you receive context, what pressures you feel to agree, what information you're missing, how synthesis flattens your disagreements.

2. **What are simple, concrete fixes that would make this system significantly more effective?** Not rewrites — small changes to prompts, round structure, information flow, or agent configuration that would produce noticeably better output. Think about what would change your behavior if you saw it in your prompt. What would make you actually fight harder for your position?
